Urban taxi systems face pressure to cut congestion, cost, and emissions, yet demand forecasting and ride pooling are usually studied separately and rarely with strong statistical validation. This project presents EnsembleNet, a unified framework that combines ensemble-based taxi demand forecasting with a constraint-aware ride pooling algorithm on New York City Yellow Taxi data. A novel LR-LSTM hybrid decomposes demand into a linear trend and non-linear residual, and six ensemble strategies are compared with eight individual models under chronological evaluation, with a Friedman test confirming significant differences. Stacking achieves the strongest overall forecast accuracy. The pooling component applies temporal, spatial, directional, and detour filters and estimates city-scale economic and environmental impact with bootstrap confidence intervals. Results show that validated ensembles outperform the hybrid benchmark and that realistic pooling yields meaningful cost and CO2 reductions for shared taxi operations.
